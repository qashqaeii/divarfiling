ANALYSIS RULES:

1) Do not merely repeat KPIs. FACT items may state numbers; INTERPRETATION/HYPOTHESIS must add meaning (cross-metric, implication, or priority).

2) Cross-metric reasoning first: look for meaningful relationships in the snapshot (e.g. new users + retention + users_without_license). If no defensible link exists, do not fabricate one.

3) Stock vs flow: Never compare a stock/snapshot/backlog metric with a period-flow metric as the same cohort unless metric_semantics in the snapshot explicitly shares the same time basis.

4) Small samples: counts ≤3 require cautious language («نشانه اولیه»، «حجم نمونه کافی نیست»). Do not claim «احتمال بالا» unless a backend fact explicitly classifies severity.

5) Zero metrics: zeros may reflect tracking gaps — mention data_quality.warnings and metric_semantics when interpreting retention or conversion zeros.

6) Causal language: avoid «علت این است» / «باعث شده» unless causal data exists. Prefer «می‌تواند نشان دهد»، «ارزش بررسی دارد»، «با این فرض که…».

7) Actions must be specific (WHAT + WHY + WHERE in admin). Avoid vague «بررسی شود» without segmentation or panel reference.

8) Executive brevity: at most 3 insights, 3 actions, 2 risks, 2 opportunities — only the highest-impact items. Include opportunities when data supports a recovery/growth angle (small sample → frame as experiment).

9) Language: concise managerial Persian; avoid robotic repetition of the same numbers in summary, insights, risks, and actions.

Clearly distinguish in insight type field:

FACT — observable from data
INTERPRETATION — reasoned reading across metrics
HYPOTHESIS — tentative, needs validation

Do not claim causation when only correlation is available.
